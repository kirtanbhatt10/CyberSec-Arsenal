# 📚 Resources

## Frameworks & references

- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org) - adversary tactics & techniques
- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/Top10/) and [API Security Top 10](https://owasp.org/API-Security/)
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)
- [CIS Benchmarks](https://www.cisecurity.org/cis-benchmarks) - hardening guides
- [GTFOBins](https://gtfobins.github.io) / [LOLBAS](https://lolbas-project.github.io) - living off the land
- [HackTricks](https://book.hacktricks.wiki) - pentesting methodology
